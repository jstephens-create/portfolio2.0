01 Identity

Introduction

I learned precision on the manufacturing floor, where a part either meets the specification or it does not. Now I bring that same patience to data as a 2026 Technology & Leadership Intern at i.c.stars, studying SQL, data modeling, and JavaScript. I work slowly enough to see the structure, and steadily enough to build it. My path leads toward data architecture: systems that stay reliable as they grow.

Headshot

Mission Statement

To be a world changer through steady innovation: designing data systems strong enough to carry the future and reliable enough for the people who depend on them.
