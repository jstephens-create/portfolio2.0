Reflect on how you’ve built habits in emotional, mental, physical, and financial wellness throughout the program.

Emotional Wellness 
Emotional wellness means being able to name, understand, and manage your emotions in healthy and productive ways. Include at least 1 of the following: 
●	A journal entry about an emotionally challenging moment

Mental wellness means managing stress, staying focused, and developing clarity and calm in the face of challenge. Include at least 1 of the following:

●	A reflection on how you’ve handled stress or burnout
